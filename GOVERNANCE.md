# Governance

FigureRelay is founded and initially maintained by **Dhruba Poudel** ([dhrubasuz](https://github.com/dhrubasuz)). Its repository is [dhrubasuz/FigureRelay](https://github.com/dhrubasuz/FigureRelay).

## Decisions

The maintainer decides scope, accepts or declines changes, and authorises releases. Public issues and pull requests are the normal record for design discussion. Security reports and personal conduct matters use an established private channel when available; see the current reporting limitations in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

The 0.1 priority is a reliable local workflow from named source metrics to reviewed, frozen report and slide outputs. Feature decisions should protect stable identity, clear provenance, predictable updates, and the exact-revision approval boundary.

## Contributions and maintainership

Contributors retain ownership and license accepted contributions under Apache-2.0. Follow the [DCO policy](CONTRIBUTING.md). Credit contributors through the repository history and appropriate notices.

Additional maintainers may be invited after sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated understanding of the project. Their scope and access should be recorded here before they receive release or repository-management privileges. No additional maintainers are appointed by this document.

## Reviews and releases

Review changes before merge. Run the relevant checks and record the actual results. Protect source/revision and approval/export semantics when changing code or storage. Review dependency licences and notices when adding or upgrading components.

A release requires a version, changelog entry, setup instructions, known limitations, verified checks, and accurate security/reporting information. Review the public files for secrets and business data before publishing. Do not describe the prototype as compliant, certified, or tamper-proof without supporting work and evidence.

## Commercial activity

Apache-2.0 permits commercial reuse under its terms. Hosted service, support, integration, and training offerings may be developed separately. Paid work does not give a contributor special authority over the community roadmap. Any future change to licensing or contribution terms must be discussed and documented, and must respect existing contributors' rights and previously granted licences.
