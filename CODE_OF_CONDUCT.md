# Community code of conduct

Maintainer: **Dhruba Poudel**.

FigureRelay welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and experience. Treat one another with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give clear, constructive feedback and acknowledge corrections.
- Respect privacy and use synthetic or redacted data in examples.
- Help newcomers understand the project and its contribution process.
- Keep discussion relevant and avoid unwanted promotional messages.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project. The maintainer may moderate content, warn participants, limit interaction, or remove access in proportion to the behaviour. Concerns involving the maintainer should use the platform's own reporting and safety processes where appropriate.

## Reporting

If you already have an established private contact with Dhruba Poudel, use that channel to report a conduct concern. A dedicated private project contact has not yet been configured. Do not publish personal information or private evidence in an issue to compensate for that limitation.

Before inviting a public community, the maintainer must publish and test a verified private conduct-reporting route and update this section. Project moderators should handle reports discreetly and share details only as needed to assess and address the concern; this is not a guarantee that information can never be disclosed.
